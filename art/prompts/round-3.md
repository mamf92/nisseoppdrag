# Nisseoppdrag – prompt pack round 3: «Spor etter nissen»

Version 1, 2026-10-20. Martin's brief for round 3:
- use the rugged, somber nisser from round 2;
- keep round 1's clear blue nights, sharp stars, moon and warm lantern light;
- add more of the fun and mystery from R-12, the porridge in the snow.

The husnisse was someone you knew was there, who helped out and sometimes played pranks, but mostly you only saw his traces.

## How the three rounds fit together

| Round | Feel | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 «Velkommen til nisseverden» | Warm, cosy, many nisser together | Onboarding, the badge medallions, the season-start moment: where the app invites you into the world |
| 2 «Stille natt» | Dark, solemn, lonely | Reference for the nisser's faces and clothes (R-01), plus R-12 and R-16, which stay |
| 3 «Spor etter nissen» | Clear starry night, warm light, mostly traces, a glimpse of him at most | Everyday screens: home, login, hatches, empty and error states, season over |

## Style in one paragraph

A clear, cold Norwegian winter night: deep but luminous blue sky, sharp white stars, a bright crescent or full moon, snow glowing blue in the moonlight. One or two warm golden lantern or window lights make pools of amber. Hand-made folk-tale illustration: ink lines with watercolour and gouache, visible brushwork on paper, but cleaner and more colourful than round 2, with no torn paper border. The nisse (R-01: old, weathered, patched grey wool, faded red cap) is rarely fully shown: a cap in a doorway, a shadow on the wall, a pair of tiny clogs under a bench. What tells the story is what he leaves behind: tiny footprints, an empty bowl, a braided mane, a prank.

## How to run it

1. **New chat** in the Nisseoppdrag project. Paste the preamble first.
2. **Attach R-01** (the round 2 character sheet) whenever the nisse appears. Attach R-12 as a style reference for the first prompt so ChatGPT sees the mood we liked.
3. **File names** start with `T-`. Upload the results to `art/round3/` in the GitHub repo (see the issue), or drop them in the design thread as before.

### Preamble

```
We are making a third set of illustrations for a Norwegian Christmas game about the fjøsnisse, the guardian spirit of the farm. You knew he was there because of what he left behind; you almost never saw him. Keep these rules for every image in this chat:
- Style: hand-made Norwegian folk-tale illustration, ink lines with watercolour and gouache, visible brushwork and paper texture, slightly rough and imperfect. Cleaner and more colourful than an old faded print. No torn paper border, no frame, the painting goes to the edge.
- Night: a clear cold winter night with a deep luminous blue sky, sharp white stars and a bright moon. Snow glows pale blue in the moonlight.
- Light: one or two warm golden lights (a lantern, a lit window, a loft door ajar) that make soft amber pools against the blue.
- The nisse (only when the prompt says so, and match the uploaded sheet): a small, very old, weathered man, knee-high to an adult, long tangled grey beard, small deep-set eyes, patched coarse grey wool, worn wooden clogs, faded red knitted pointed cap. Serious or sly, never grinning at the viewer. Usually only partly seen: from behind, half in shadow, peeking.
- Mood: quiet mystery with a twinkle of humour. Cosy because of the warm light, not because of cute characters.
- Never: glossy, plastic, 3D render, airbrushed, cartoon, cute big eyes, rosy apple cheeks, greeting-card perfect, Thomas Kinkade glow, Santa Claus, pointy elf ears, text, letters, numbers, signatures or watermarks.
Reply "Klar" and wait for my first prompt.
```
