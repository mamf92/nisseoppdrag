Illustrations for Nisseoppdrag, generated for this project and exported by the design thread
(AVIF and WebP). `badges/` holds the fifteen medallions at 256 px (lists) and 512 px (the full-view coin), `family/` the five Gården portraits.
The licence for the artwork is separate from the code and is not decided yet.
`gate/` has the login paintings (L-11 for Gården, R-11 for Låven) and `story/` the two story cards (L-12, R-10), each as AVIF and WebP at 1x and 2x.
`home/` has the Home paintings for when no week is open: R-13 and L-13 between weeks, R-14 and L-14b when the season is over.
`portraits/` has the 19 player portraits (128, 256 and 512 px WebP), heads cropped from the character sheets L-01 to L-07 and R-01 by `scripts/portraits/build_portraits.py`.
`categories/` has the five task category medallions (C-20A to C-24A, social, kindness, learning, collaboration, holiday_fun) at 128 and 256 px, AVIF and WebP; prompts in the design folder's `prompt-pack-categories.md`.
